# StudySpace Privacy Policy — beta review draft

The current in-app notice is served at [`/privacy`](https://studyspace.wiki/privacy) from `src/app/privacy/page.tsx`. The sign-up form requires acknowledgement of this notice and records the accepted privacy version and timestamp in the new user's signup metadata.

The notice describes account and profile information; courses, notes, assignments, quizzes, calendars and student records; uploaded coursework; AI prompts, selected study context and generated responses; technical/security telemetry; Supabase authentication and database storage; Vercel hosting; configured AI providers; optional Cloudflare Turnstile; browser session storage; and guest-session recovery tokens.

## Must be completed before broad public release
- Confirm and publish the operator's legal identity, address/jurisdiction and privacy contact.
- Inventory the exact active AI providers/models and disclose their processing regions, retention, subprocessors and data-use/training settings.
- Confirm Supabase/Vercel regions, analytics, cookies, monitoring providers and any other third-party integrations.
- Verify account deletion, backup expiry, AI telemetry and support-log retention periods.
- Define the privacy-request process for access, correction, export and deletion.
- Confirm age eligibility and applicable Canadian/provincial and other privacy-law obligations with qualified counsel.

This remains a beta draft, not legal advice or a claim of legal review. Do not represent it as finalized until the blockers above are resolved.
