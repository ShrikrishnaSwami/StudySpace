# StudySpace Terms of Service — beta review draft

The current in-app draft is served at [`/terms`](https://studyspace.wiki/terms) from `src/app/terms/page.tsx`. The sign-up form requires explicit acceptance and records the accepted terms version and timestamp in the new user's signup metadata.

The terms cover the student-only beta service, accounts and guest access, acceptable use, student-owned content, AI-generated material and grading limitations, availability/data-loss limitations, account termination, and changes to the service.

## Must be completed before broad public release
- Confirm and publish the legal operator's full name and service address.
- Confirm minimum age and parental/guardian consent requirements for the jurisdictions where students may sign up.
- Add a verified support/contact address and a governing-law/dispute clause approved for the operator's jurisdiction.
- Have qualified counsel review consumer-protection, liability, content-license, termination and dispute language.
- Confirm account deletion, export and backup timelines match the actual implementation.

This remains a beta draft, not legal advice or a claim of legal review. Do not represent it as finalized until the blockers above are resolved.
