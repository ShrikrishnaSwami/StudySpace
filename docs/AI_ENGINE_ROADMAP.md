# StudySpace AI — public roadmap

StudySpace uses AI to support learning workflows. The goal is to make study tools more useful while keeping students in control of their work.

## Current areas of focus

- **Academic explanations:** help students explore concepts and ask follow-up questions.
- **Workspace context:** use relevant supported study material when available.
- **Practice generation:** assist with quizzes, flashcards, and review activities.
- **Coursework support:** provide guidance and feedback for supported assignment workflows.
- **Syllabus assistance:** identify possible dates and tasks from supported syllabus content.
- **Study recommendations:** help students organize learning and plan next steps.

## Principles

- AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. Students should check important results against course instructions and trusted sources.
- AI assistance should support learning rather than replace a student's own judgment or instructor guidance.
- Private student content should be handled only for the features and purposes described to users.
- The system should communicate errors clearly and avoid presenting unsupported information as fact.
- AI features should be tested for quality, reliability, accessibility, and appropriate usage.

## Roadmap themes

1. Improve the clarity and usefulness of academic responses.
2. Make source context and limitations easier to understand.
3. Improve handling of unsupported files and ambiguous input.
4. Evaluate generated practice material and feedback for correctness.
5. Improve reliability during provider errors and periods of high demand.
6. Collect beta feedback responsibly and use it to prioritize improvements.

This public roadmap intentionally excludes internal prompts, private architecture, implementation details, operational limits, provider configuration, and security controls. Future work is subject to beta feedback, technical feasibility, and available budget.
